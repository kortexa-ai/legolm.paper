Table~\ref{tab:bpb} reports unpaired adaptation at 3000 steps. Across 9 host/modality pairs, the true-minus-constant BPB differences range from -0.0010 to 0.0005; 9 of their 9 intervals include zero. These within-host comparisons test whether variable features are needed under independent text/sensor sampling. Intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalence, and this experiment does not rule out a likelihood benefit from conditioning on paired sensor/text data. Static adapters provide a sensor-free text-adaptation reference. For true-minus-static BPB, 5 intervals are above zero, 2 below zero, and 2 include zero. Negative differences favor the bridge; the ordering is not uniform. Figure~\ref{fig:bpb} includes every training corruption at both budgets; the machine-readable summary retains the paired intervals, and the appendix reports the separate common-byte panel.

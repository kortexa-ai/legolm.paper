Table~\ref{tab:resources} reports measured costs, including the frozen encoder. For successful training trajectories, accumulated optimizer-step time and peak allocated memory are Mini 5.24 accumulated optimizer-step hours and 1.94 GiB peak allocated training memory; LFM 230M 12.20 accumulated optimizer-step hours and 5.04 GiB peak allocated training memory; Qwen 0.8B 76.44 accumulated optimizer-step hours and 17.58 GiB peak allocated training memory. Step times include batch preparation and synchronized training. They exclude model loading, checkpoint writes, separate evaluation, and failed attempts. The artifact also records each successful worker attempt's wall time. Concurrent workers overlap, so the sums are neither isolated GPU-hours nor total study time. The measurements include the dense output head's cost.

# Paper 1 publication protocol

Work unit: [legolm.paper #1](https://github.com/kortexa-ai/legolm.paper/issues/1).

Paper 1 has a self-contained reproduction package under `paper1/`, including
its numerical method contract, dependency lock, immutable result references,
and manuscript-generation commands. All papers remain on `main`. Existing
reproduction directories keep their own commands and dependency environments.

Publication requires a standalone installation, artifact-integrity verification,
full declared experiment coverage, and manuscript regeneration from the released
evidence. Model execution and artifact-only verification are separate workflows.
The historical root harness remains available with its original result artifacts.

The release includes only public scientific material. Reviews, operational logs,
private planning, machine credentials, and unrelated experiments are outside the
export. The owning issue records validation and delivery evidence.
